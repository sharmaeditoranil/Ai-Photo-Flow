02

AI Culling & Quality Assessment

During wedding shoots, photographers capture thousands of rapid frames. The Culling Engine automatically categorizes photos into BEST, PICK, REVIEW, or REJECT.

Four-Stage Decision Pipeline:
Each image is analyzed across four orthogonal computer vision dimensions:
1. Laplacian Sharpness Metric

Evaluates high-frequency spatial gradients. Distinguishes artistic lens bokeh from camera shake or missed focus.

2. Eye & Blinking Status

Detects faces and computes the Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR). Automatically flags closed eyes, mid-blink expressions, and squinting.

3. Dynamic Range & Clipping

Inspects highlight blowouts (bridal jewelry, silk whites) and shadow crush (groom tuxedo, black suits).

4. Composite Quality Score

Synthesizes all measurements into a unified score (0 to 100). Scores above 85 are classified as potential AI Best winners.

03

Duplicate Burst Clustering & AI Best Shot

When photographers shoot bursts (3 to 10 frames of the same moment), Ai PhotoFlow clusters them using a 3-layer perceptual similarity algorithm: 

// Perceptual Burst Clustering Rule:
1. Timestamp Delta (Δt ≤ 3.5s)
2. Perceptual Structural Hash (dHash & pHash 64-bit vector correlation)
3. HSV Normalized Color Histogram Intersection (Similarity ≥ 0.90)
The "AI Best Only" Rule:
In the AI Best gallery view, only the single top-ranking winner of each burst set is displayed. Secondary duplicates are automatically hidden and routed to the Similar view, keeping the main gallery clean and clutter-free!

AI Color Science & +5% Vibrance Boost

Western auto-editors frequently ruin Indian wedding photos by turning warm skin orange or over-saturating bridal attire. Ai PhotoFlow solves this with dedicated color science algorithms:

Restricts corrections to human skin chrominance coordinates (Cr: 133–173, Cb: 77–127) to preserve natural warmth without reddish/magenta casts.

Pure Light White Balance

Neutralizes fluorescent greens, yellow halogen spill, and cyan LED stage lights without washing out festive mandap fire ambiance.

+5% Universal Vibrance Boost

Every edited photo receives a calibrated +5% baseline vibrance boost. Boosts flowers, lehengas, and backgrounds while protecting skin.

Hardware Fingerprinting & Anti-Crack Protection

To prevent pirated copies and unapproved distribution, Ai PhotoFlow implements cryptographic hardware locking:

Motherboard UUID + CPU Hash

Binds the software activation strictly to the physical motherboard and processor identifiers of the workstation.

HMAC-SHA256 Signatures

License keys (FLOW-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) are cryptographically validated offline using mathematical secret-salt signatures.

Anti-Clock Rollback

Detects unauthorized system clock tampering to prevent users from artificially resetting trial expiration timers.
